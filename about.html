About Stephen

A clinical practice built around children and their parents.

Stephen Cross is an accredited member of the Ontario Association of Family Mediators. He serves Oakville, the Greater Toronto Area, and beyond.

Stephen has particular expertise in high-conflict separation and divorce. His private practice provides specialized therapeutic services for families who have been impacted by separation and divorce.

He has over 30 years of experience helping families resolve parenting disputes. For sixteen years he served on the clinical panel of the Office of the Children’s Lawyer, a branch of the Ministry of the Attorney General, where he prepared reports for the court and provided clinical assistance to children’s lawyers.

Stephen has strong clinical skills in family functioning and a solid foundation in trauma, loss, attachment theory, and permanency. He is committed to the work, has great respect for the diversity of every person, and takes pride in an anti-oppressive practice.

He has a special interest in working with families who are multicultural, multi-ethnic, and multi-faith. He also has experience working with First Nations families, and with LGBTQ youth and families. He views each family through a lens of anti-oppression, equity, and inclusion.

Stephen spent many years in the child welfare sector, working primarily with children in care and their caregivers. Many of those children had significant needs and had suffered trauma, abuse, and loss, often with multiple caregivers. He has extensive experience interviewing children of all ages, from preschoolers to teenagers, including children with learning or developmental disabilities, autism, behavioural or mental health issues, or medical and physical disabilities.

He has been qualified by the courts as an expert in his field and has testified in the Ontario Court of Justice and the Superior Court of Justice on numerous occasions.

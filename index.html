The practice

Services focused on separating and separated families.

Divorce and separation are rarely simple, and this is often the hardest period in a family’s life. Mediation cannot solve every case. It is successful in most, and it gives parents a more affordable and peaceful way to separate without damaging dignity or children.

Stephen Cross is a neutral guide. He does not make the decisions for you. He helps you negotiate terms you can live with, in a process that stays confidential, constructive, and focused on the children.

Family mediation

What working together looks like.

Other services follow their own path. This is the shape of a typical mediation.

01

Individual meetings

Stephen meets each person first. He explains the process, hears each perspective, and considers whether mediation is appropriate and whether any safeguards are needed.

02

Joint sessions

Ground rules are set, issues are named, and options are explored. Children’s views can be brought in when that would help the matter move forward.

03

A written agreement

When you reach agreement, Stephen drafts a memorandum and reviews it with you. Your lawyers can turn that into a legally binding separation agreement.

Reintegration counselling

Rebuilding contact, carefully.

Reunification counselling re-establishes contact between a parent and a child when that relationship has been disrupted, or when a long stretch of time has passed with no contact.

Parents sometimes need professional guidance for a child who is alienated from a parent, or who is caught between the parents. A child may show distress or other symptoms during transitions between homes. Parents may need help with how to communicate with each other, or a place to consult about the dispute itself.

Intensive therapy with the family — both parents and the children — is central to re-establishing contact in a healthy and safe way. Stephen may work individually with the children and with each parent, in whatever combination is recommended.

The purpose

The therapy works with each parent and the children toward appropriate relationships. It includes education so that each parent can understand the needs of each child, and the repercussions for a child — in childhood and later as an adult — of a severed relationship with a parent.

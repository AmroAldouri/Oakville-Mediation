One-cost solution

Know the cost from the beginning.

Streamline Collaborative Divorce is a one-cost solution. It is built for people who want peace of mind about what the divorce will cost, from the start through to the signing of the separation agreement.

A good fit for

  • Clients who want peace of mind, knowing the cost of their divorce from the beginning to the signing of the separation agreement.
  • Clients who want to engage the right professional for the right job.
  • People who need to resolve their separation agreement.

Streamline collaborative sits alongside Stephen’s work as a family professional in collaborative practice. If you want to know whether this model fits your file, call the office and Stephen can talk it through with you.

Parent education and coaching

Tools for a new kind of parenting.

Coaching may be with one parent, or with both parents together. The aim is to give parents tools, support, and structure so they can be a steady resource for their children through the transition.

Advice and guidance cover a range of issues related to separation and divorce, at any point during the separation or after it.

That can include how to tell a child about an upcoming separation, what children’s reactions to divorce often look like, and which dispute-resolution services are likely to fit the family’s needs.

  • Individual parent coaching.
  • Co-parent coaching.
  • Guidance before, during, or after the separation.
  • Help choosing the right next service for the family.

Services offered

Support matched to the dispute in front of you.

Mediation, coordination, assessment, and therapeutic work for families living through separation and divorce. Choose the service that fits, or call and Stephen can help you sort out where to begin.

01

Family mediation

A voluntary, confidential process. Stephen is an unbiased neutral who will not decide for you. He helps parents negotiate the terms of an agreement themselves.

Read

02

Collaborative practice

A client focused alternative to court. Each person has a lawyer, and a family professional and financial professional join the team.

Read

03

Streamline collaborative

A one cost collaborative divorce for people who want the cost known from the beginning through to the signing of the separation agreement.

Read

04

Parenting plans

A written plan for raising children after separation: parenting time, transitions, and how decisions will be made.

Read

05

Voice of the child

A neutral report that brings a child’s views, wishes, and preferences into mediation, arbitration, or litigation.

Read

06

Parenting coordination

Coaching and facilitation after a parenting plan or court order, when parents still struggle to reach day to day agreements.

Read

07

Reintegration counselling

Therapy to reestablish contact when a child’s relationship with a parent has been disrupted or has lapsed.

Read

08

Assessments

Parenting assessments, usually under section 30 of the Children’s Law Reform Act or on consent, for matters that need a full clinical picture.

Read

09

Parent coaching

Education and coaching for one parent, or for co parents, at any point during or after separation.

Read

Counselling

Specialized therapeutic services for families who have been impacted by separation and divorce.

Financial mediation

Offered in collaboration with a financial divorce specialist, for division of assets, finances, or child support.
